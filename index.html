Made in code for LUNAR

The arcade starfield, universes and the 1,000 events are drawn in code. Rover terrain is generated in code from each world’s real gravity and colors: Earth is a small island with grass, trees, a lake and the sea, Mars is open sand dunes, and the Moon is cratered regolith. None of them is a map of a real location, and the trees, grass and water are simple shapes made for LUNAR. Distances and sizes in the Solar System view are compressed so you can see everything at once.
